# FRD-001 — High-Risk Transaction Review

Transactions with a combined fraud risk score of 0.65 or greater require review by an authorized fraud analyst. Transactions scoring 0.85 or greater must be temporarily blocked in the synthetic environment until a human reviewer records a decision.

Evidence must include the model score, relevant behavioral deviations, entity relationships, and any contradictory signals. Automated decisions must not expose raw customer identifiers in generated reports.
